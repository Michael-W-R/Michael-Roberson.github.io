Background

My path to software runs through the classroom and the back office. I spent seven years as an educator, then moved into public-sector roles working hands-on with HR management and general-ledger accounting. Seeing how much time those functions lose to manual, error-prone processes is what drew me to technology.

Today I focus on building tools that create efficiency and accuracy — replacing repetitive, error-prone work with systems people can rely on, and turning fragmented data into decisions leaders can act on.

Summary

I build software that solves real operational problems for Soldiers and Families. My work spans Army HR-modernization tools — Power BI data models and Power Apps workflows that automate evaluations, rating chains, and readiness reporting — and independent projects like PCS Scout, a live public web app I designed and shipped end-to-end.

I'm a largely self-directed learner: I taught myself data modeling, DAX, and full-stack web development by building tools people actually use. I care about reducing friction, encoding complex policy into reliable logic, and turning fragmented data into decisions leaders can act on.

As a University of Texas at Austin alum, I'm especially drawn to the Army Software Factory's mission in Austin — building modern software inside the force, for the force.
