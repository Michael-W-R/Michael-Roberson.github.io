PCS Scout

Flagship · Live

A public web app I designed and built end-to-end to help Soldiers and Families find housing during a PCS move. It pulls official DoD BAH rates, models rush-hour commute times, maps "fair-commute" zones between two workplaces, estimates VA-loan home affordability, and surfaces nearby schools — then hands off to listing sites pre-filtered to the user's budget. No account, no ads, no tracking.
